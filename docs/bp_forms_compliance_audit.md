# FY2027 BP Forms compliance audit

Audit date: 14 October 2026 (Australia/Brisbane). Baseline: `origin/main` at `c6d44ae`. Implementation branch: `feature/fy2027-bp-forms-audit`.

**Status: partial implementation, not full DBM compliance.** BMS is an internal preparation, review and consolidation workflow. Its generated working schedules do not reproduce every official template. Submission to BMS is not submission to DBM. Official layouts, agency certification, reference-code validation and the final DOST allocation of responsibility remain outstanding.

## Financial compatibility and reference data

- Internal monetary values are **pesos**, to two decimals; aggregation uses integer centavos. Official thousand-peso layouts require an explicit divide-by-1,000 presentation layer, not a database-unit conversion. Current printable schedules explicitly say pesos and internal working schedule.
- New primary classes are PS, MOOE, FINEX and CO. FINEX is an internal stable class token, not a government UACS code. The seed and startup upgrade add a Financial Expenses reference row without modifying existing category IDs.
- Climate amounts are a subset of MOOE/CO expenditure, bounded by their underlying item. They are reported separately and never added to the grand total. Typology text is captured without claiming membership of an authoritative code list.
- Historical TAG rows retain their category ID, text and amount. Their amount remains included exactly once in historical/request/consolidation totals. The display flags their classification as unresolved. TAG does not trigger BP207. No bulk conversion, deletion, deduplication or automatic inference is authorised. New TAG expenditure rows cannot be created through the request API; existing TAG rows can be saved unchanged and any deliberate classification change is an explicit edit.
- The grand total can exceed classified PS + MOOE + FinEx + CO totals when old TAG entries remain. This is an explicit unresolved difference, not a reason to quietly assign them to MOOE. A formal historical reconciliation/migration requires DOST's decision.
- PREXC/UACS, appropriation source and OU are reusable capture fields. No invented government codes, salary grades, financing fund codes, typology categories, exchange rates or historical amounts are inserted. Presence of a typed code does not mean it is validated against official reference data. Linking imported verified reference datasets is still required.

## Selection and responsibility policy

The administrator and Step 2 share stable question IDs, wording, type, answer options and stored values. Funding source is the same request value in Steps 1 and 2. Conditions save question ID/answer, support ALL/ANY, and retain old field/operator/numeric/aggregate-foreign conditions. Numeric legacy conditions are displayed as saved rules; administrators replace them with proposal questions rather than exposing a technical expression editor.

The default automatic triggers remain **implementation approximations**, even where the purpose/classification of the form is officially verified. In particular, Tier 2 does not prove every BP202 profile is needed; foreign funding does not prove all FAP profile details; nonpermanent staffing needs further eligibility interpretation. Administrators can configure per-year rules, levels, fields and permitted removal overrides. Disabled forms are skipped. An administrator mandate cannot be removed unless overrides are explicitly permitted; valid changes retain reasons and actor-backed activity records.

Requester forms, institutional/consolidation schedules and forms needing office contribution are identified separately through level, explanation and completing-office text. Institution forms remain available; agency-labelled forms are **not all converted to requester requirements** or assigned exclusively to Central Office. DOST must confirm per-form responsibilities and timing. No mandatory HR/Finance user lanes are introduced. A form not selected by the current conditions is not a submission requirement, while its previously entered draft values remain retained.

## Lifecycle, validation and security

- Drafts use the current fiscal-year configuration on save/reopen; submissions independently re-evaluate the server's saved configuration. Configurations for different years remain separate. A changed fiscal year recaptures the correct definitions.
- Draft-only partial rows are retained in `draftLineItems`; financial totals and conditions use persisted valid financial items, not client-provided totals. Answers and custom values for inactive forms are retained. Signed/supporting files survive saving and reopening.
- Each submission captures complete built-in/configured form definitions, values, common fields, item-derived totals and lines, reasons, overrides and attachment identities. Review and consolidation no longer rewrite the source submitted snapshot. Later configuration/code changes do not alter captured definitions.
- Signed copies bind to rendered form content and definitions. Changing relevant fields/common data/items/definitions requires replacement. Older unversioned signed copies remain accessible but must be reuploaded before a new unified submission. Existing-file metadata cannot be rewritten to make an old signature appear current. This detects content changes; it does not verify handwriting or legal authenticity of a scan.
- The API validates built-in and configured required fields, relevant question answers, amounts, item ownership, signed file existence and required supporting documents. New request creation and edits require a requester assigned to the owning institution/unit. Submitted records are locked, repeated submissions rejected, review actors come from the authenticated token, and cross-request item/version IDs are rejected. Previously submitted records cannot be deleted through draft deletion.
- The mock token/login system remains a **demo authentication mechanism**, as documented in README. It is not production authentication. Laravel must implement real authentication, storage authorisation and the same validations; this work does not turn the mock server into a production service.
- The Forms module's older saved requests remain readable. Pre-unified records retain their historical workflow; new API-created records capture unified configuration. Existing historical records without captured built-in definitions cannot retroactively be made immutable/exact by inference.

## Remaining delivery limits

The implementation materially improves the workflow but is **not a complete reproduction of the FY2027 BP set**. Full official multi-period/Tier/OU/UACS tables, finance-system reference feeds, statutory compensation/retirement calculations, true signed-document verification, exact government-form pagination and automatic official consolidated forms remain incomplete. Institution working schedules, the existing Forms module, consolidated financial reporting and signed-upload requirements are preserved. The itemised gaps above are intended to support client review and subsequent implementation without fabricating policy.
